〇〇を〇〇します

In English and Spanish, the verb comes in the middle

  • I read a book → S V O
  • Yo leo un libro → S V O

In Japanese, を marks the direct object. The object comes before the verb

  • 私は本を読みます → S O V
  • を = direct object marker (marks what the verb acts on) — No direct equivalent in English
  • 私は本を読みます。→ I read a book.
  • パンと卵（たまご）を食べます。→ I eat bread and an egg.

何を

  • 何を = what (as object)
  • あなたは明日何をしますか。→ What do you do tomorrow?
    • →明日サッカーをします。→ I play soccer tomorrow.

〇〇で〇〇を〇〇します

  • Combine で (place of action) with を
  • メキシコでかばんを買（か）います。→ I buy a bag in Mexico.
  • どこでその映画（えいが）を見（み）ましたか。→ Where did you see that movie?
  • あなたは東京で何をしますか。→ What do you do in Tokyo?

〇〇に〇〇をします

  • に = at (specific time) — Used with specific times: 10時に、月曜日に
  • 明日10時に映画を見（み）ます。→ I will watch a movie at 10 tomorrow.
  • あなたは明日何をしますか。→ What will you do tomorrow?
  • ※ 今日・明日・昨日など、relative time words do NOT take に.

〇〇しましょう

  • 〇〇しましょう = Let's do 〇〇 (suggestion to do together)
  • 休みましょう。→ Let's take a break.
  • 勉強しましょう。→ Let's study.
  • ※ The verb ending changes depending on the verb group. We'll cover conjugation rules in a later lesson.

いっしょに〇〇しませんか

  • 〇〇しませんか = Would you like to do 〇〇? (polite invitation)
  • Difference from しましょう:
    • しましょう → "Let's~" (speaker assumes agreement)
    • しませんか → "Would you like to~?" (leaving the choice to the listener)
  • 一緒（いっしょ）にお茶（ちゃ）を飲（の）みませんか。→ Would you like to have tea together?
  • ※ The verb ending changes depending on the verb group. We'll cover conjugation rules in a later lesson.
